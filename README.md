🤝 Mitra – AI-Powered Student Community Platform

Mitra is an AI-enabled student community web application designed to connect college students through academic resources, housing information, collaboration tools, and intelligent AI assistance.

The platform combines social networking, academic sharing, real-time communication, and AI-based tools into one unified ecosystem to support students throughout their academic journey.

Unlike traditional fragmented student resources (separate platforms for notes, housing, messaging, and marketplace), Mitra integrates everything into a single intelligent platform.

Students can:

discover PGs and shops near campus

upload and download subject-wise academic notes

communicate using real-time chat

access AI-based study assistance

buy and sell items in a student marketplace

showcase college projects

stay updated with campus events

🌍 Project Vision

University students often face several problems during their academic journey:

difficulty finding reliable PG accommodation

lack of organized study resources

limited peer collaboration tools

absence of a central student community platform

lack of AI-driven learning support

Students typically rely on multiple disconnected platforms such as WhatsApp groups, Google Drive, or informal networks.

This creates inefficiencies in information sharing, collaboration, and learning.

Objective

Mitra aims to create a smart digital ecosystem for students capable of:

connecting students in a community-driven platform

providing AI-powered academic assistance

enabling resource sharing and collaboration

simplifying student life management

supporting mental health awareness and support

🔎 Key Features
🏠 PG & Local Shop Listings

Students can easily discover accommodation and nearby shops.

Features include:

detailed PG listings

rent and facility information

location-based filtering

shop listings near campus

Database currently contains 40+ PG and shop entries.

📚 Academic Notes Repository

Students can upload and download subject-wise notes.

Capabilities:

structured notes upload

categorized by subject

peer knowledge sharing

easy download access

Currently hosting 200+ study resources.

🤖 AI Study Assistant

The platform includes an AI-powered chatbot and study assistant.

Capabilities:

answer academic questions

simplify complex notes

generate summaries

provide study explanations

Accuracy: ~95% response relevance

Built using:

LangChain

LLM APIs

Transformer models

🧠 Mental Health Emotion Detection

Mitra integrates AI-based emotion analysis to support student mental wellness.

Capabilities:

detect emotional tone from user text

provide mental health support prompts

guide students toward helpful resources

Model used:

DistilBERT transformer

🗺 Skills Roadmap Generator

Students can generate time-based learning roadmaps for specific skills.

Example:

Input: "Learn Web Development"

Output:

Month 1 → HTML, CSS
Month 2 → JavaScript
Month 3 → React
Month 4 → Backend Development

This helps students plan career-oriented learning paths.

💬 Real-Time Student Chat
